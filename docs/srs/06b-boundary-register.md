# 6. Security: boundary register

## 6.3 Boundary register

Every ParleyInn component, process and protocol, with its boundary or why it is
rejected (SEC-19). The set is closed, as the
[domain model](../domain-model/components-and-surfaces.md) lists it. No
packaging (OQ-32) may change a row. "Disable" names the SEC-22 power that
covers the row: "yes" where managed policy can disable the row, else the
boundary or the launcher whose disabling covers it.

A ParleyInn feature whose purpose is to reach off the machine through a tool the
principal runs outside ParleyInn, such as its own tunnel, MUST have its own row,
be
off by default and be turned on by a widening principal act (SEC-19); row 11 is
one. Room trailers and trailer links (row 28, always on, LANE-28) are not such a
feature, nor is a head or purge receipt, which carries only digests (VIEW-10).
An "outside" row names what leaves through a tool ParleyInn does not run: the
harness, or the principal's own tunnel or `git push`. Rows 8, 10, 19 and 20 rest
on who signs and seals what their component records (open: OQ-40); rows 11 and
17 rest on a paired phone's keys and writer and on the phone-scoped room-view
secret (open: OQ-39).
