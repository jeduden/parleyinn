| OWN-11 | P1  | Every principal act MUST belong to exactly one class, as the [domain model](../domain-model/acts-and-roles.md) lists them: cut, neutral or widening. Any principal act that removes a pin from a restore block or stops this node recording its own runs' events MUST be widening whatever verb carries it, except an unstamp or a trust-grant revocation, which withdraws only the acting principal's own trust (LANE-32, OWN-29). A cut or neutral act MUST be recorded with a mark naming its principal surface, and any free text in it MUST be recalled as untrusted whatever its event's trust level.
