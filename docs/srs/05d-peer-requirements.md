# 5. Functional requirements: peer network

## 5.14 Peer network (PEER)

Being a peer never makes content trusted: what a peer brings in is
trusted only as PRV-02 classifies it.

| PEER-01 | P2  | Sync MUST run only in the peer component (B2), off by default. Turning it on is a widening principal act the CLI records, and turning it off a cut one: unlike a revocation (PRV-10), it refuses no event and drops no pin, and what other devices record meanwhile arrives once it is on again (PEER-03); its own entry point, started by the person, a service manager or an ephemeral node's entrypoint, never by a core process, MUST run only while that act stands. On an ephemeral node, the principal's write-once environment variable MUST stand in for that act, minting the node's access token being the widening act; the environment variable MUST be recorded as a structural event, not a principal act, and audited. Whether it ships in the core's executable or its own MUST NOT change its boundary. With it absent or stopped, every other requirement MUST be met as with no peer component.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | T    | I4, I9  |
| PEER-02 | P2  | Every peer MUST be able to hold complete copies of the rooms it shares and serve each room's segments to the enrolled peers whose principal has a seat in that room (LANE-16) and to the blind peers that hold the room (PEER-12), and to no other (open: OQ-41), so a personal room's segments go only to its principal's other nodes, as do its cross-room posts until OQ-41 closes; from any other room's writers but a personal room's, a peer MUST get only a cross-room post, by its address, when a seat of its principal in the room the post targets asks for it (LANE-29). A token-key-only node MUST get only the segments of the rooms its token key is limited to (PEER-06) and the cross-room posts they show. No peer MAY be required for two others to exchange segments, and no third-party service for enrollment, discovery or relay (CON-06).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | T, D | I4      |

| PEER-05 | P2  | A node marked ephemeral MUST seal and offer its open segment to a connected peer at every turn end and subagent end its transcripts record, at its harness session's end, and at least every 30 s† while a run is active. A connected ephemeral node MUST offer each sealed range as soon as it is sealed. A writer MUST be marked retired by a principal act or by the expire act ending the access token whose token key certified its seat key; once its clock passes that access token's expiry, the node that minted it MUST record that expire act, or, before PRV-10 ships, refuse every later use of the access token. Either act MUST name the writer's last accepted `seq`, and no derivation reads a clock. Only the principal a writer's seat key chains to MAY retire it by a principal act (before PRV-10 ships, only one recorded on the node whose device key certified that seat key or the token key that certified it); any other principal act retiring it MUST be void and shown. A peer MUST still accept its segments past that `seq` that continue its chain without a fork, marked delivered after retirement (open for the room merge: OQ-41), and only a revocation refuses them. A retired writer's lost tail, or that of a writer a new seat replaced (REC-24, ADM-06), MUST be shown as a missing range after the last `seq` received, never as a quiet end or as `behind`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | T    | I1, I6  |
| PEER-06 | P2  | Enrollment MUST verify the key on both nodes, by matching words or a scanned code; discovery MUST NOT enroll. An access token for certifying an ephemeral node's seats MUST be scoped to rooms, to creating new rooms or continuing named ones, and to an expiry. It MUST carry the bound identity of each repository the node works in (LANE-02), a token key, certified by the issuing node's device key and limited to those rooms, the node's own personal room and the expiry, with the digest of its recovery key, which the issuing node keeps, naming the token key it succeeds, if any (PRV-12), and with which the node certifies its own seat keys without reaching any peer, and every enrolled peer network address it may deliver to. A token key signs no principal act, so every pin the node writes MUST restore only once a principal stamps it from one of its devices whose device scope allows it (PRV-10), no trust grant covers its posts or pins (OWN-29), and the node's configuration is never accepted (ADM-04; open: OQ-06). Minting an access token that names a room to continue MUST be its principal's ask for each run the node records to join that room at the run's start, as the room's admission permits (LANE-23), and MUST say so; the access token MUST carry that ask as a signed event, and that room's qualifying pins (PIN-10) as signed events keeping their provenance (`operator` for a device seat's pin, `assistant` for a run seat's), and until a peer is reached the node's restore blocks MUST say later pins may be missing. The node's principal MAY choose, by a configuration setting under ADM-04, that the node reads its access token from an environment secret or a mounted file; `parinn status` MUST name where it reads the access token from, and ParleyInn MUST remove the access token from the environment of child processes. Minting an access token MUST be a widening principal act and revoking one (`parinn access-token revoke`) a cut one unless it removes a pin from a restore block, which makes it widening (OWN-11); its issue, use, rotation and revocation MUST be audited, and the issuing node MUST show an access token no writer used as `enrolled, never synced`. | T    | I6, I8  |

| PEER-09 | P2  | Presence and typing hints MUST be ephemeral, never stored in the record, and attributed only to the peer's device key, or a token-key-only node's token key, that authenticated the connection. | PEER-10 | P1  | Wherever ParleyInn holds two different events at one (writer, seq), with no network needed, it MUST refuse the later head's segment under REC-21, keep it unrewritten for forensics, and let the first head seen win. This applies to `parinn verify` over the segments the node holds, a backup restore (ADM-06), a room bundle import (REC-23) and a peer's sync. Each refusal MUST be audited and counted, and `parinn verify` MUST report the conflict with the integrity status `refused` (VIEW-10) and MUST NOT offer `parinn fork choose`. Before seals ship (REC-18, M7) nothing authenticates a copy (OQ-48), so a differing event may be mere tampering and the writer is not marked. Once both events are sealed by the writer's key, ParleyInn MUST keep both, mark the writer `equivocated`, and stop every derived artifact for that writer at the fork until the node's principal chooses which fork to keep (`parinn fork choose`), a widening principal act. The refusal ships with ADM-06 and REC-21 in M3, the bundle import's check with REC-23 in M9, the `equivocated` mark with the seals in M7, and the check between peers with the peer network in M8.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | T    | I6, I10 |
| PEER-11 | P2  | Every purge (ADM-07, ADM-14) MUST be sent as an erasure request, and every quarantine as a quarantine request, to every enrolled peer holding the room, as signed events, `operator`, or `structural` for an erasure request a retention policy sends. A peer's principal applies an erasure request by a widening principal act (OWN-11), and a quarantine request by one of the class of the quarantine it applies (SEC-12), refusing either by a neutral one, and a peer that applies one MUST run SEC-31 on every copy it holds in any writer. Each peer's state (applied, refused or unreachable) MUST be audited, counted and shown. A peer that refuses keeps its copy, and the room view MUST say so.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | T    | I5, I6  |
| PEER-12 | P2  | A peer MAY be enrolled as a blind peer, for storage only. The node's principal MUST enroll a blind peer for a room only as the room's visibility allows (LANE-17). A blind peer MUST hold and serve a room's sealed ranges only encrypted to the device keys of the room's principals, or a token-key-only node's token key where the rooms it is limited to (PEER-06) include that room, never to seat keys, and MUST verify the seat key's signature over each encrypted range before storing it. It MUST learn nothing beyond writer ids, address ranges, sizes and signatures: no event content, payload, header field, commitment key or room metadata. It MUST NOT derive room state, have a seat in the room, or carry a principal act's authority. The room's principals MUST decrypt only on their own nodes. A change of the room's principals MUST encrypt every range sent afterwards to the device keys of the principals after the change; ranges a removed principal's nodes already received stay readable there, and the room view MUST say so. Every surface MUST mark a blind peer as one.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | T    | I4, I8  |
| PEER-13 | P2  | A bar (LANE-25) MUST take effect on the node that records it as soon as it is recorded, and the peer component MUST stop sending the room's segments to every key the bar covers. A node MUST show every missing range in a writer it holds that can carry room acts beside the room's membership, never as a quiet end. A bar MUST stop only future content, and the room view MUST say that a barred seat keeps what its node already holds.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | T    | I6, I8  |
