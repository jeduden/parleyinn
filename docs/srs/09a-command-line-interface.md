A verb that takes a room act (`parinn room create`, `join`, `leave`, `kick`,
`bar`, `unbar`, `mute`, `unmute`, `post`, `link`, `present`, `pick` and `set`,
`parinn room-summary write`, by the facilitator's device seat (LANE-33), and
`request`, `parinn role request`, `parinn pin add`, `edit` and `unpin` of a
device-seat pin of a type that does not restore or written from a token-key-only
node, a verdict excepted, and `parinn list-removal make`) signs it with the seat
key of the principal's own seat in that room (LANE-23, LANE-24) and, but the
facilitator's program's (SEC-32), refuses unless its standard input and output
are a terminal (OWN-12). Create room, join, a join request and leave are checked
against admission and the add, every other room act against the seat's role
(LANE-24). The owner's device seat, but a paired phone's, has every room
capability, for room acts and the principal acts that need one, but writing a
room summary; its room acts edit and unpin only pins its principal wrote from a
device seat that do not restore unstamped, and make a list removal of any pin
but the intent or a verdict, while the owner's agents' run seats have only their
role and any appointment (LANE-16). Room acts carry none of OWN-11's classes: on
their own they never widen what reaches an agent, which only principal acts do,
and no pin, edit, unpin or list removal room act changes any restore block
(LANE-24, LANE-31, LANE-32).

Every other verb that records an act takes a principal act, signed at the
principal surface by its device's device key once PRV-10 ships, and recorded on
that device's device seat in the room it acts on, which the node first joins
without admission when its principal has a member seat there (OWN-02, LANE-31);
`parinn ingest`, `import`, `rebuild`, `migrate`, `canary`, `doctor`,
`backup create`, `head-receipt make`, `ui`, but `ui --phone`, and `launch`
record no principal act, and `install` and `uninstall` only the acts their rows
name. One that acts on no room or on a room its principal has no member seat
in, or that a paired phone signs, goes to that device seat in the personal room,
naming the room, which shows it by address (LANE-29). Each passes OWN-12 in its
OWN-11 class, the same on every principal surface. A verb marked widening also
needs the controlling terminal and, where required, a presence proof (OWN-11).
No verb takes an expire act: a node records it with its device key (LANE-25).

`held-request answer` is the verb for held requests, `verdict record` for
verdicts, and `witness-check start` for confirming a command taken from an
untrusted event (OWN-18), so one word never means two acts. Every command that
starts a B1–B3 component, but `parinn ui` and `parinn launch`, names that
component; SEC-01 applies however the components ship (OQ-32). Every address
argument accepts each form the domain model lists under
[Address](../domain-model/record.md), the ASCII input form included. Every
command MUST support `--json` output and MUST use documented exit codes (0
success, 1 failure, 2 usage error, 3 integrity failure).
